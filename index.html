11AM – 2PM
Shimokitazawa — Vintage Clothing in Yukata
20 min from Asakusa (Tobu → transfer → Odakyu to Shimokita). Tokyo's undisputed vintage capital — 6 shopping streets, 760+ stores. Priority shops: Flamingo (curated American vintage and Japanese streetwear), New York Joe Exchange (quality secondhand, good English), Stick Out Vintage. Wearing yukata here is genuinely special — locals will love it and photos are exceptional. Drop larger bags at coin lockers at Shimokita Station (¥400–600).
Many shops CASH ONLY — bring yen Coin lockers at station entrance Most shops open 11AM Wearing yukata here = perfect photo moment

3:30 – 5:30PM
Sakurai Japanese Tea Experience — Spiral Building, Aoyama
5th floor of the Spiral Building, Minami-Aoyama (20 min from Shimokita). Only 8 counter seats. Founder Shinya Sakurai prepares flights of 30+ varieties of Japanese tea — gyokuro, hojicha, matcha, and rare regional teas. Tea courses from ¥5,500/person (~$34). Evening bar program features tea-infused cocktails starting at 6PM. Matthew: this is the serious tea moment of the July trip — not a tourist ceremony but a genuine sensory education. Air-conditioned, intimate, unhurried. 90-min session.
RESERVATION REQUIRED — book now: sakurai-tea.jp/en English staff · AC · 8 seats only ¥5,500/person (~$34) for tea course 5F Spiral Building · Opens 11AM
